Assistant Professor
Division of Electronics & Electrical Engineering, Dongguk University

Welcome! I am an Assistant Professor at Dongguk University, Seoul, South Korea. My research develops artificial intelligence methods for multimodal medical data analysis — particularly imaging together with clinical text — with a focus on interpretable, generalizable models that improve diagnostic accuracy, support surgical decisions, and enhance patient outcomes. More recently I have been working on Agentic AI systems in which coordinated agents enable autonomous, transparent, and reliable clinical decision support, and on Physics-Informed Neural Networks for chemo-mechanical simulation, bridging AI with physical system modelling for AI-PHM and Physical AI applications. Before joining Dongguk, I worked as a Machine Learning Engineer at AirFi in Barcelona, Spain, and as a Lecturer at Superior University in Lahore, Pakistan.

Research

Interpretable and generalizable AI for multimodal medical data, autonomous clinical decision support, and physics-informed modelling of physical systems.

My work builds AI methods for multimodal medical data analysis, combining imaging with clinical text to improve diagnostic accuracy and support surgical decision-making. Current directions extend this toward agentic AI systems, where coordinated agents reason autonomously and transparently over clinical data, and toward physics-informed neural networks that embed governing physical equations directly into learning.

Current Research Projects
🔬
Agentic AI · Consumer Electronics
Agentic AI for Autonomous and Interpretable Colorectal Polyp Segmentation

A consumer-electronics-oriented agentic AI framework for polyp segmentation and clinical decision support, designed to operate across hospital workstations, mobile devices, and patient-facing consumer electronics.

🧬
Segmentation · Multimodal LLM
Adaptive Edge and Context Fusion with Language-Informed Diagnostic Reasoning

EMCAF-Net, an Edge-Aware Multi-Scale Contextual Attention Fusion Network for accurate cell nuclei segmentation in cancer diagnosis, integrating edge-aware multi-scale fusion for boundary localization with a multimodal LLM for automated diagnostic reasoning.

🔋
Physics-Informed ML
PINNs for Coupled Lithium Diffusion and Stress Simulation in Battery Particles

A physics-informed neural network framework for the coupled chemo-mechanical PDEs governing lithium transport and stress evolution in battery electrode particles, replacing traditional finite element methods. Anisotropic, SOC-dependent diffusivity tensors derived from molecular dynamics simulations are embedded as inputs, enabling accurate modelling of irregular particle geometries from X-ray tomography data.
